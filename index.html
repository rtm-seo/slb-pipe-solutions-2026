Win

Organic search drives the leads

Organic search brought 37% of sessions but 128 of 160 key events (80%). Organic key events rose from 22 in Q4 2025 to 41 in Q3 2026, and July (15) and September (14) were the two best months of the year. Call tracking backs this up: Website SEO calls rose from 111 in Q4 2025 to 155 in Q3 2026 (+40%).

Win

Rankings moved up

Average position improved from 38.1 to 31.0 between the first and last quarter. September posted the best full-month position (26.8) and click-through rate (0.35%), and October is tracking at 26.2 so far.

Watch

Impressions fell in Aug and Sep

Impressions dropped to 93,281 in August and 57,440 in September while clicks held steady. The loss came from "near me" searches ranking below position 20 (about 46K impressions a month in May to Jul, 11K in Aug to Sep). Impressions from top-10 rankings grew over the same span.

Fix

Bot traffic is inflating GA4 totals

2,728 Direct sessions came from Singapore and China at about 10% engagement. That is why total sessions grew while site-wide engagement fell from 53% to 31%. Organic engagement stayed near 60%.

08Recommendations

Ranked by expected impact on leads.

  1. Push the CIPP and trenchless pages onto page oneHigh

    The Indianapolis CIPP service page has 100K impressions at position 34 and already sits at 6 to 10 for "cured in place pipe liner contractors," "cipp lining companies near me," and "cipp contractors near me." Work contractor and company wording into its H1 and opening copy, add project photos and lining specs, and point internal links from the CIPP blog posts to it. Do the same for the Indianapolis trenchless page, which ranks 5.4 for "trenchless sewer repair."

  2. Make the CIPP page rank for "sewer line repair Indianapolis"High

    The CIPP Pipe Lining & Relining page does cover this service. The body says SLB can "repair your sewer line" in one day, and one H2 reads "Fix Your Sewer Line in One Day!" What it lacks are the exact phrases people search: "sewer line repair," "sewer line replacement," and "sewer repair" never appear, and the title tag, meta description, and both H1s only say CIPP pipe lining and relining. Google ranks the homepage for these searches instead: "indianapolis sewer line repair" at 23.7, "sewer line repair indianapolis" at 18.6, and "indianapolis sewer pipe repair" at 13.2. The CIPP page shows up only for "indianapolis sewer pipe repair," at 34.0. Add "sewer line repair" to the title tag, meta description, and opening paragraph, work "sewer line repair and replacement" into the "Fix Your Sewer Line in One Day!" section, combine the two H1s into one, and link to the page from the homepage with "sewer line repair in Indianapolis" as the anchor text.

  3. Tighten the Bloomington camera inspection pageMedium

    It ranks 7.4 for "drain line camera inspection in bloomington" and 12 to 15 for related terms with no clicks. Sharper title and meta copy plus local proof (neighborhoods served, sample inspection findings) should lift CTR.

  4. Refresh high-impression blog postsMedium

    "Understanding the CIPP lining process" (58K impressions, position 54) and "The best ways to repair cast iron pipes" (31K, position 33) are seen often but rarely clicked. Update them with answer-first intros, current pricing ranges, and links to the matching service pages so they also feed AI Overview citations.

  5. Send municipal blog readers to the municipal service pageMedium

    Several municipal articles already rank on or near page one: "sewer line repair for municipalities" (9.2), "cctv sewer inspection" (9.8), "municipal manhole to manhole lining" (12.7), and "uv pipe lining for municipalities" (15.1), with about 1,950 impressions last quarter and one click. Add a clear call to action and a link from each post to the Municipal Trenchless page, and sharpen their titles so city and utility buyers see SLB as a contractor rather than an information source.
